An unofficial map of 566 bus stops and 31 routes in Keichiku (Yukuhashi City, Kanda Town, Buzen City, Chikujo Town, Yoshitomi Town, Miyako Town, Koge Town). Main operators: Nishitetsu Bus Kitakyushu, Kanda Town, Taiyo Kotsu, Chikujo Town, Buzen City, Buzen City & Yoshitomi Town & Nakatsu Cityand others. Based on FY2022 data + GTFS (Nov 2026) data; timetables and fares are not included.
