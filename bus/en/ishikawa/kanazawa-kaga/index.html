Main operators: Hokutetsu Noto Bus, Hokutetsu Kanazawa Bus, Uchinada Town, Kaetsunou Bus, Hokutetsu Kanazawa Bus & Hokuriku Railroad (Hokutetsu), West Japan JR Bus Companyand others.
