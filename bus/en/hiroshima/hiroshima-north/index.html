About bus routes and bus stops in Northern Hiroshima

An unofficial map of 1,802 bus stops and 134 routes in Northern Hiroshima (Shobara City, Kitahiroshima Town, Miyoshi City, Akitakata City, Akiota Town, Hiroshima City). Main operators: Okuizumo Kotsu, Shobara City, Bihoku, Chugoku Bus, Miyoshi City, Akitakata Cityand others. Based on FY2022 data data; timetables and fares are not included.
